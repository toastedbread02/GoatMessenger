Cookie & storage policy

Our cookies so far: zero. No analytics, no ad trackers, no pixel, no third-party script — the site has nothing to put in one. This page tells you what is stored on your machine, because honest storage beats a dark-pattern "Accept All" wall.

1. This website

When the small banner at the bottom of the pages asks about cookies, it is recording your choice: one entry (gm:cookieChoice) in your browser's localStorage, plus the date it was made. That's it. Declining changes nothing about this site, because it sets nothing either — but you asked for the choice, so you get it, with two equally sized buttons and no "Necessary cookies" excuse-trap.

2. The web app (Continue in browser)

Running the app in your browser stores everything on your own machine:

  • IndexedDB — your decrypted conversation history, your ratchet state, your preferences, your per-chat verification marks. Namespaced per account.
  • localStorage — username of the last signed-in goat, chosen theme, developer-mode flag, cookie choice. No tokens, no identity material.
  • Clearing it: deleting the account (Settings → Profile → Delete account & data) wipes the app's storage in one go. Or clear site data in your browser whenever you want — everything simply resets.

3. The desktop app (macOS / Windows)

The desktop builds put data where an operating system keeps it properly:

  • Session + private identity live in the OS keychain (macOS Keychain / Windows Credential Manager) so "stay signed in" never touches disk in the clear.
  • Chat history and preferences live in the app's local user-data folder.
  • Clearing it: Settings → Privacy → Clear local history, or sign out, or delete the account entirely for a full wipe.

4. The relay (server side)

The relay stores no cookies either — it speaks HTTP with bearer tokens, no session cookies at all. What it does store is in the privacy policy, §3: account basics, contact relations, queued ciphertext and encrypted uploads. Its owner — for a self-hosted relay, that means you — can read that folder anytime.

5. Third-party trackers

None. Loading this site fetches: the page, a stylesheet, one PNG goat, one script (the banner, served from the same domain), and screenshots. The web app is served from the same GitHub Pages origin. There is no CDN fingerprinting you, no font beacon, no "HMRC-approved" analytics to discover later.
