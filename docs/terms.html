Terms of service

This is a free, open-source hobby project. The terms are short because the deal is simple: you use the code as it is, you own your relay, and you don't break the law with it.

1. What you're using

GoatMessenger is end-to-end encrypted messaging software ("the app") plus a relay server program, licensed under the license in its repository, maintained by @toastedbread02. There is no paid tier, no subscription, and no hidden upsell — there isn't anything to upsell.

2. Provided "as is"

THE APP AND THE SITE ARE PROVIDED AS-IS, WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED — INCLUDING FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE. If a ratchet misbehaves, the goat chews a bug: file it. Nothing sold, no contract of supply, no SLA.

3. Your passphrase is your responsibility

Your keys are derived from your passphrase and are not recoverable by anyone — including us. That is the point of the design. If you lose your passphrase and never saved your groups, you lose the account. Please keep it written somewhere a stable goat would.

4. Age

Accounts require confirming you are 13 or older (and older where the law requires). Guests are one-tap temporary sessions meant for trying the app, and they carry the same rules.

5. Responsible use

  • Don't use GoatMessenger to break your local or international laws — no harassment, no illegal content, no distributing malware, no attempts to crack other herds' accounts.
  • Don't attack the relay you use: no spam floods, no scraping the user list, no DoS. He is a goat, not a bot farm.
  • Bans are per-relay: if you run one, you can rate-limit or refuse connections freely; there is no global authority here to appeal to. That's the trade-off of self-hosting.

6. No affiliation

GoatMessenger is not Signal, not affiliated with any other messenger brand. The cryptography (X3DH + Double Ratchet) follows published public protocols; any bugs in it are ours and ours alone.

7. Availability

Messages relay only while both goats are online to the same relay. If your relay is off, your queue waits. We cannot restore accounts or data; there is nothing stored anywhere that we control. The privacy policy explains exactly what each relay holds.

8. Changes

These terms change when the code changes — each release is its own agreement. No deactivation emails, no fine print buried eleven clauses deep; you just read five goat haiku's worth above.
